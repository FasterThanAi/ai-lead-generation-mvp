# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive experience for everyone. Contributors are expected to act professionally and in the best interests of the community.

## Expected Behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate of others.
- Communicating constructively and accepting different perspectives.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback professionally.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respecting the project's guidelines and decisions.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing private or sensitive information about others without permission.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or other inappropriate conduct.
- Threats, intimidation, or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and interactions where someone is representing or participating in the project, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other community channels.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it to the project maintainers through the contact or reporting channels provided by the repository. Please provide enough information for the maintainers to understand and investigate the concern. Reports will be handled as respectfully and confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may take appropriate action in response to behaviour that violates these guidelines, including removing comments or contributions, restricting participation, or taking other measures they consider appropriate.

Maintainers will aim to handle reports fairly and consistently while protecting the privacy of those involved.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is based on common open-source community standards and is intended to support a welcoming and respectful project environment.
